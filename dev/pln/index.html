5. Fitting the Poisson-lognormal layer

The latent log abundances $Z_{ij}$ of §2 are not observed. The likelihood of the counts requires the integral

\[p(y_{ij} \mid \mu_{ij}, \sigma_j^2) = \int \text{Poisson}\big(y_{ij};\, e^{o_i + z}\big)\, \mathcal N(z;\, \mu_{ij}, \sigma_j^2)\, dz ,\]

which has no closed form. Following Chiquet et al. (2018), the package maximises instead a lower bound of the log-likelihood, the ELBO, by variational EM.

5.1 The variational approximation

  • approximation — the posterior of each latent value is replaced by an independent Gaussian: $q(Z_{ij}) = \mathcal N(M_{ij}, S^2_{ij})$.

With a Gaussian $q$, every term of the ELBO is explicit, because $\mathbb E_q[e^{Z}] = e^{M + S^2/2}$:

\[\text{ELBO} = \sum_{i,j}\Big[ y_{ij}(o_i + M_{ij}) - e^{\,o_i + M_{ij} + S^2_{ij}/2} - \frac{(M_{ij} - \mu_{ij})^2 + S^2_{ij}}{2\sigma_j^2} - \tfrac12\log\sigma_j^2 + \tfrac12\log S^2_{ij}\Big] + \text{const}.\]

The algorithm alternates two steps, each of which increases the ELBO.

5.2 E-step: a closed form with the Lambert W function

For one cell, the ELBO is concave in $M$ and in $S^2$. Result — its maximum in $M$ has a closed form:

\[M_{ij} = \mu_{ij} + \sigma_j^2 y_{ij} - W_0\!\Big(\sigma_j^2\, e^{\,o_i + \mu_{ij} + \sigma_j^2 y_{ij} + S^2_{ij}/2}\Big),\]

where $W_0$ is the principal branch of the Lambert W function ($W e^W = x$). The variance solves the fixed point $S^2_{ij} = 1 / (e^{\,o_i + M_{ij} + S^2_{ij}/2} + 1/\sigma_j^2)$, a contraction. Three alternations of the two updates are enough.

Derivation

Setting the derivative in $M$ to zero: $y - e^{a + M} - (M - \mu)/\sigma^2 = 0$ with $a = o + S^2/2$. Write $u = \mu + \sigma^2 y - M$. The equation becomes $e^{a + \mu + \sigma^2 y - u} = u/\sigma^2$, that is $u\,e^{u} = \sigma^2 e^{a + \mu + \sigma^2 y}$, so $u = W_0(\sigma^2 e^{a + \mu + \sigma^2 y})$. For large counts the argument overflows, so $W_0(e^t)$ is computed directly by Newton's method on $w + \log w = t$, from $w = t - \log t$. The derivative in $S^2$ gives $-\tfrac12 e^{a+M} - 1/(2\sigma^2) + 1/(2S^2) = 0$.

The E-step is a loop over the $n \times p$ cells, run in parallel over genes.

5.3 M-step: the Gaussian machinery

With $q$ fixed, the ELBO depends on the model parameters only through

\[-\sum_{i,j} \frac{(M_{ij} - x_i^\top B_j - w_i^\top\Gamma_j)^2 + S^2_{ij}}{2\sigma_j^2} - \frac n2 \sum_j \log\sigma_j^2 .\]

This is a Gaussian factor regression of the matrix $M$ on the design, solved with the rotation of §3. The $k$ design rows of $Q^\top M$ give $B = R^{-1}(Q^\top M)_{1:k}$ exactly. A weighted PCA of the $n - k$ residual rows gives the factors $W$ (orthogonal to $X$) and the loadings $\Gamma$, with the offset factor first. The overdispersions are the residual sums of squares, plus the variational variances:

\[\sigma_j^2 = \frac{1}{n - k - q - 1}\sum_i \Big[(M_{ij} - \mu_{ij})^2 + S^2_{ij}\Big],\]

shrunk across genes by vash or limma (§4.3).

5.4 From the PLN fit to MOUTHWASH

The confounded estimate of each gene is the rotated row of interest of $M$: $\hat{\tilde\beta}_j = q^\top M_{\cdot j} / R_{22}$, with $q$ the column of $Q$ for the covariate of interest. Its standard error needs care.

Result — the residual rows of $M$ cannot provide it: the E-step pulls them towards the fitted factors, so their spread underestimates the noise. On simulations this gave a false discovery proportion of 25 % at a nominal 5 %.

Instead, the standard error uses the model's own variance of each cell on the latent scale: the lognormal overdispersion plus the Poisson noise, $v_{ij} = \sigma_j^2 + 1/\lambda_{ij}$, where $\lambda_{ij} = e^{o_i + \mu_{ij} + \sigma_j^2/2}$ is the expected count:

\[s_j = \frac{1}{\lvert R_{22}\rvert}\sqrt{\sum_i q_i^2\, v_{ij}} .\]

Then $\hat{\tilde\beta}_j$, $s_j$ and the PLN loadings $\Gamma$ go into MOUTHWASH (§4.2). Without confounding, this standard error needs no inflation ($\hat\lambda \approx 1$) and 95 % intervals cover 95–96 % of the true effects (§7.3).

Functions

  • pln: variational EM for the PLN model with design, factors and offset factor.
  • pln_mouthwash: PLN fit followed by MOUTHWASH.
